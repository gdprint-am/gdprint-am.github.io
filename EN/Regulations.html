Regulations

1. General Provisions

1.1. These regulations establish the terms of use for printing services provided through the website, as well as the rights and obligations of the user and the service provider.

1.2. By using the website or placing an order, the user confirms that they have fully read, understood, and unconditionally accept all the terms of these regulations.

1.3. If the user does not agree with any provision of these regulations, they are obliged to cease using the website.

1.4. An order placed through the website is considered a legally binding electronic transaction.

2. Description of Services

2.1. The printing house provides printing services, including:

● large format printing

● plotter cutting

● sale of self-adhesive films

● production of advertising materials

● other printing services

2.2. The printing house has the right to change or discontinue the list of services at any time.

2.3. The printing house undertakes to ensure print quality by using appropriate materials and equipment but is not responsible for technical or design errors in files provided by the client.

3. Placing an Order

3.1. Orders are placed exclusively through the website by filling out the appropriate form.

3.2. The user is obliged to provide accurate and complete information.

3.3. The printing house has the right to reject an order if:

● the files violate the law

● they contain offensive or illegal material

● they infringe on copyright

3.4. Order fulfillment deadlines may change depending on the volume of work, technical conditions, or force majeure situations.

4. Responsibility for Files

4.1. The client bears full responsibility for the content of the files provided for printing.

4.2. The printing house is not responsible in the following cases:

typos

color differences

incorrect dimensions

incorrect layout

if these are present in the files provided by the client.

4.3. Printing done by the printing house is considered accepted if the client receives it without submitting a written complaint.

5. Payment

5.1. Order fulfillment begins only after full payment is received.

5.2. Payment is made using the methods indicated on the website.

5.3. The paid amount is non-refundable if the work has already been started or completed.

6. Delivery

6.1. Delivery cost is calculated based on distance, starting from 250 drams per km.

6.2. For orders of 50,000 drams or more, delivery is free unless other conditions are specified at the time of order.

6.3. The printing house is not responsible for damages caused by third parties during delivery.

7. Limitation of Liability

7.1. The website is provided on an "as is" basis.

7.2. The printing house is not responsible for:

temporary inaccessibility of the website

technical failures

damages resulting from internet or software issues

7.3. The total liability of the printing house is limited to the value of the specific order.

8. Prohibited Content

The printing house prohibits orders containing:

● violations of RA laws

● hate speech

● illegal advertising

● copyright infringement

● counterfeit trademarks

● sexually explicit images or depictions of nudity

● child pornography or any content inappropriately involving children

● hate speech, incitement to violence, or aggressive content

● materials infringing copyright without permission

● trailers or bloody, tragic, or other unacceptable shocking images

The printing house has the right to reject any order that does not comply with these rules and to take measures in accordance with the law.

9. Intellectual Property

9.1. All content of the website is protected by copyright.

9.2. Without written permission, it is prohibited to:

copy website materials

use the design

use the software code

9.3. In case of violation, liability is established by the legislation of the RA.

9.4. In case of violation, compensation ranging from 100,000 to 500,000 drams, as well as legal liability, may be applied.

10. Force Majeure

The printing house is not responsible for non-fulfillment of obligations if it is due to the following circumstances:

● natural disasters

● war

● power outage

● internet outage

● government restrictions

11. Changes to the Regulations

11.1. The printing house has the right to change these regulations at any time.

11.2. Changes come into effect from the moment they are published on the website.

11.3. Continued use of the website constitutes acceptance of the new terms.
